What the app does

Shore ID uses an AI model to identify objects in photos you take on the beach and gives general information about them, including safety, rarity, value and whether you can take them home.

Identifications can be wrong

The app's answers are generated automatically and are provided for general interest only. They may be inaccurate or incomplete. In particular:

  • Safety: never rely on the app to decide whether something is safe to touch, handle or eat. Some marine animals and washed-up objects are dangerous even when they look harmless. If in doubt, leave it alone. If something could be ordnance, a flare or a chemical container, move away and contact local emergency services.
  • Value: value notes are rough ranges, not valuations or offers.
  • Taking things home: guidance is general. Laws on collecting shells, fossils, wildlife and other items vary by country, region and beach, and you are responsible for checking and following local rules and signs.

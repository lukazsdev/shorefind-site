Shore Identifier

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Shore Identifier (shown as "Shore ID" on your phone; also known as Shorefind) is a beach identification app: you photograph something you found on a beach and the app tells you what it is. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it. Shore Identifier is made by an independent developer, lukazsdev, who is responsible for this data ("we", "us"). Questions: lukazsdev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No sign-up. The app works with a random account ID created on your device.
  • Photos you scan are sent to our server and to an AI provider to identify the object, then stored so you can see them again in My Finds.
  • We only ever use your approximate location as a country and region name. Exact coordinates never leave your phone.
  • Apple or Google handle payments, and RevenueCat manages subscriptions. We never see your payment details.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites. We don't sell data.

What the app collects and why

Photos you scan

When you tap the shutter or choose a photo, the app shrinks it and sends it to our server, which sends it to Anthropic's Claude AI to identify the object. The photo and the identification result are then saved to your account so they appear in My Finds. Photos are stored in a private bucket that only your account can read. We don't use your photos to train anything. Anthropic processes the image to generate the answer under their API terms and does not use API inputs to train their models.

Country and region

If you allow location access, the app looks up your approximate position on the device and converts it to a country and region name (for example "Cornwall, United Kingdom"). Only that text is sent with a scan, so the answer to "Can I take it home?" can reflect local rules. Exact coordinates are not stored and are not sent to us. The app works fully if you decline.

Questions you ask the expert

If you use "Ask the expert", your question, the identification it relates to, and the answer are sent to Anthropic's Claude to generate the reply and are stored with your account so you can see them again. Notes and favourites you add to finds are stored with your account too. Delete my data removes all of it.

Your quiz answers

During onboarding we ask what you hope to find and how often you visit the beach. These are saved with your account so we can tune tips. They are not used for advertising.

Your subscription

If you subscribe, the App Store or Google Play takes the payment. RevenueCat, our subscription provider, receives your random app user ID, your purchase and subscription records from the store, and basic device information (device model, operating system, app version, country and IP address) so it can tell the app and our server whether your subscription is active. We don't receive your name, Apple ID, Google account or payment details.

Technical data

Like any internet service, our hosting providers see your IP address when the app connects and keep it in short-lived server logs for security and troubleshooting. We also count how many scans each account makes per day to prevent abuse.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: our database, file storage and server functions.
  • Anthropic: the AI model that identifies your photo, in the United States.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management, in the United States.
  • Apple and Google process payments under their own privacy policies.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. Transfers outside the UK and EU are covered by standard contractual clauses.

Legal basis

If you're in the UK or EU: we process this data to provide the app you asked for (contract), and to keep it secure and working (our legitimate interests). Location is processed only with your consent, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings.

How long we keep it

  • Photos and results: until you delete the find, use Delete my data, or your account is removed.
  • Accounts that haven't been used for 12 months may be deleted along with their photos.
  • Subscription records: kept by RevenueCat while needed to manage your subscription and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your choices and rights

You can delete any find by long-pressing it in My Finds, and delete everything with Settings → Delete my data, which removes your photos, results and quiz answers from our servers straight away. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing lukazsdev@gmail.com. Because the app never asks who you are, we may not always be able to match an emailed request to your data; Delete my data always works. You can cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

If you're in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Safety information

Identifications and safety ratings are generated by an AI model and can be wrong. Never rely on the app to decide whether something is safe to touch or eat. If something looks like it could be dangerous, leave it alone and contact local authorities.

Children

Shore Identifier isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If the way the app uses data changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

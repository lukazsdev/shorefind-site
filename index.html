Shore Identifier

Found something on the beach? Snap it. Know it.

Shore Identifier (shown as "Shore ID" on your phone; formerly Shorefind) identifies shells, sea glass, shark teeth, fossils, jellyfish and more from a photo, and tells you whether it's safe to touch and whether you can take it home.
